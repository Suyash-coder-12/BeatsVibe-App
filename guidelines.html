Community Guidelines

Fostering a culture of builders, not just coders.

1. The BeatsVibe Ethos

At BeatsVibe Technologies, we are building an elite community of engineers, architects, and founders. Our forums, Discord servers, and AI interactions are professional environments. We expect every node (student) to operate with respect, curiosity, and integrity.

2. Academic Integrity & Anti-Cheating

When participating in hackathons, assignments, or certification exams, your code must be your own. While we encourage the use of NeuroNova AI as a mentor to understand logic, directly copy-pasting solutions without comprehension violates our academic integrity policy.

3. Zero Tolerance for Toxicity

We maintain a strictly zero-tolerance policy against:

  • Harassment, bullying, or discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or technical skill level.
  • Gatekeeping or mocking beginners. Every architect started by writing "Hello World".
  • Spamming the forums with irrelevant content or unauthorized promotional material.

4. Constructive Code Reviews

When reviewing peers' code during internships or community projects, provide actionable, constructive feedback. Use professional language and focus on the logic and architecture, not the individual.

5. Enforcement Actions

Violations of these guidelines will be reviewed by our administration team. Consequences may include a warning, temporary suspension of forum privileges, or permanent termination of your BeatsVibe account and revocation of certifications.
