Executive Director

Suyash Rathod

Founder & CEO, BeatsVibe Technologies

"The future of technology belongs to those who understand the full spectrum of engineering. We are not just teaching syntax; we are building architects capable of bridging hardware, scalable cloud systems, and artificial intelligence."

The Architectural Vision

Suyash Rathod is the visionary force behind BeatsVibe Technologies. Blending a rigorous academic foundation in Electrical Engineering with deep, practical expertise in modern DevOps and Full-Stack Architecture, Suyash approaches technology education from a systems-level perspective.

Before scaling BeatsVibe, Suyash served as a Junior DevOps Developer for the Revenue Department, Government of Maharashtra. There, he gained critical experience managing complex CI/CD pipelines and securing high-stakes digital infrastructure. This enterprise-level exposure shaped his belief that tech education must be rooted in real-world deployment, not just local environments.

A passionate educator at heart, Suyash also spent time as an IT and computer programming instructor in Pune, mentoring students at institutes like the Anees Defence Career Institute. This hands-on teaching experience highlighted the glaring gaps in traditional tech syllabuses, leading to the creation of the BeatsVibe ecosystem—a platform where students learn to build, deploy, and scale.
