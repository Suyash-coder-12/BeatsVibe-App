Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2026 | Effective Date: August 25, 2026

Welcome to BeatsVibe Technologies ("Company", "we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this massive privacy notice or our practices with regard to your personal information, please contact us.

This Privacy Policy describes in exhaustive detail how we collect, use, process, and distribute your information when you use our services, including our website, NeuroNova AI learning systems, and any related software applications (collectively, the "Services"). We take your privacy with the utmost seriousness. Please read this privacy notice carefully as it will help you understand what we do with the information that we collect.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information You Disclose to Us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, participate in activities on the Services (such as enrolling in our engineering, MERN stack, or AI courses), or otherwise contact us. The personal information we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and may include:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, username, educational background, college name, and professional titles.
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone numbers, and billing addresses.
  • Credentials: Passwords, password hints, and similar security information used for authentication and account access.
  • Payment Data: We collect data necessary to process your payment if you make purchases, such as your payment instrument number and the security code associated with your payment instrument. All payment data is stored and processed directly by our payment processor, Razorpay. We do not store your raw credit card numbers.

B. Automated Information Collection and Telemetry

We automatically collect certain information when you visit, use, or navigate the Services. This information does not reveal your specific identity (like your name or contact information) but may include device and usage information, such as your IP address, browser and device characteristics, operating system, language preferences, referring URLs, device name, country, location, and information about how and when you use our Services.

C. AI Interaction Data (NeuroNova Integration)

Because BeatsVibe Technologies utilizes advanced artificial intelligence (NeuroNova AI) to enhance your learning experience, we collect specific data related to your interactions with this system. This includes:

  • Code snippets, queries, and terminal commands you input into the NeuroNova widget.
  • Your learning trajectory, module completion rates, and performance analytics to dynamically adjust curriculum difficulty.
  • Bug resolution times and error logs generated during your coding sessions to improve our AI's diagnostic capabilities.

2. How We Process Your Information

We process your personal information for a variety of critical business and educational reasons, depending on how you interact with our platform. We rely on valid legal bases to process your data, including your consent, the necessity of fulfilling a contract (delivering courses to you), and our legitimate business interests.

  • To facilitate account creation and authentication: We process your information to manage your account securely.
  • To deliver and facilitate delivery of Services: Processing enrollments, generating certificates, and unlocking modules.
  • To train and optimize NeuroNova AI: Anonymized interaction data is used to improve the accuracy, responsiveness, and educational value of our proprietary artificial intelligence models.
  • To protect our Services: We use IP mapping and security telemetry to prevent fraud, unauthorized account sharing, and DDoS attacks against our servers.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing and Disclosure

We only share information with the following third parties under strict confidentiality agreements and data processing addendums (DPAs). We do not sell your data to data brokers.

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Firebase, Vercel, and Hostinger for database hosting and server provisioning.
  • Payment Processing: Razorpay for secure financial transactions and subscription management.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics and internal telemetry tools to understand user flow and platform scale.

4. Data Security and Encryption Protocols

We have implemented robust, enterprise-grade technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. This includes AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, strict access control protocols for our system administrators, and regular penetration testing of the BeatsVibe core architecture. However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA, & DPDP Act)

Depending on your geographical location, you possess comprehensive rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a complete cryptographic copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request corrections to any inaccurate data via your Student Dashboard.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the termination of your node (account) and the deletion of your personal data from our active databases.
  • Opt-Out of AI Training: You may request that your specific code snippets and queries be excluded from the aggregate training dataset for NeuroNova AI.

6. Data Retention Policy

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this privacy notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as tax, accounting, or other legal requirements). When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will either securely delete it or anonymize it.
